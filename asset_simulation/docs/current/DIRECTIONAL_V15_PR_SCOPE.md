# Directional v1.5 PR scope

This candidate supersedes the v1.4 experiment. It does not merge any change into `main` by itself.

The intended review boundary is deliberately narrow:

1. move direct capital sizing ownership out of the shared PM policy and back to the Investment Decision Committee plus strategy-specific risk adapters;
2. preserve the directional thesis hardening with required `minimum_direction_forecast_z`;
3. reduce forecast hidden-truth transmission so economic cooling occurs at the information source rather than through a cross-strategy leverage haircut;
4. validate style ecology on separate development and validation seed sets.
